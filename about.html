Our mission

Engaging, accessible, relevant

EUSA is a student-led club that makes endocrine science engaging, accessible, and relevant. We explore everything from health and performance to metabolism, stress, sleep, and disease through education, interactive events, and community. EUSA is a place to learn beyond the classroom, meet science-minded peers, and get real exposure to medicine, research, and wellness.

What is endocrinology?

Endocrinology is the branch of biology and medicine that studies hormones and the glands that make them, like the thyroid, adrenals, pancreas and pituitary.
